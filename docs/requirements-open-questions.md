# Requirements — Open Questions

All questions to clarify with mom before development begins. To be filled after the interview.

> **Status:** ⏳ Awaiting mom's interview

## Medical Details

- [ ] Does she have doctor-prescribed daily carb or calorie limits?
- [ ] How many meals per day does her doctor recommend?
- [ ] What is her target blood sugar range (fasting / post-meal)?
- [ ] Are there any other health conditions we haven't accounted for?
- [ ] Does she take any medications that interact with food (e.g. metformin)?
- [ ] What is her specific fat limit per meal (gallbladder)?

## Eating Habits

- [ ] How many times a day does she currently eat?
- [ ] What are her 10–20 most commonly eaten foods or ingredients?
- [ ] Does she cook the same dishes regularly? Which ones?
- [ ] What does she snack on between meals?
- [ ] Are there any foods she definitely avoids (allergies, intolerances)?
- [ ] Does she already keep a food journal? What format?

## Daily Schedule

- [ ] What time does she usually wake up?
- [ ] What time does she go to sleep?
- [ ] Are her mealtimes fixed or flexible?
- [ ] Does she leave the house during the day? (affects phone/food access)

## Tech Comfort

- [ ] What phone does she use (Android / iPhone)?
- [ ] Is she comfortable opening links in the browser?
- [ ] Would she want to save the app to her home screen?
- [ ] Does she already use Google Sheets on her phone?
- [ ] How large should buttons and text be?

## Goals & Preferences

- [ ] What does she find most annoying about tracking food manually now?
- [ ] What would be the single most useful feature for her?
- [ ] Would she like reminders when it's time to eat?
- [ ] Any other wishes or ideas?
